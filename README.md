**Night Vault** — a top-down stealth game. You are Moth, a cat burglar in the Halcyon Museum after dark. Slip past patrolling guards and their vision cones, hide behind crates and display cases, melt into the shadows, toss a coin to pull a guard away, steal the Star Sapphire and reach the green exit unseen. Three levels, a detection meter that gives you a chance to recover, and a Ghost rating if nobody ever raises the alarm.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Click (or press Q) to toss a coin and distract a guard. P pauses, R restarts, Enter starts.

## Hand it in

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
